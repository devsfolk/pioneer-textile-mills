Manufacturing Process

A seamless journey from raw cotton to finished fabric.

01 Step 01

Spinning

Our spinning division is equipped with Rieter (Switzerland) and Toyota (Japan) machinery. We process the finest Pakistani cotton to produce ring-spun carded and combed yarns.

  • Blow Room & Carding
  • Drawing & Simplex
  • Ring Spinning & Winding
02 Step 02

Weaving

We operate a versatile weaving unit comprising Air-Jet (Tsudakoma) and Sulzer Projectile looms. This allows us to weave fabrics in widths up to 126 inches with various weaves like Percale, Satin, and Twill.

Capacity:

1 Million Meters / Month

03 Step 03

Dyeing & Finishing

Our processing unit ensures vibrant colors and soft hand-feel. Processes include singeing, desizing, scouring, bleaching, mercerizing, dyeing, and sanforizing.

  • Continuous Dyeing Range
  • Rotary Printing
  • Stenter Finishing
