Our Quality Standard

At Pioneer Textile Mills, quality is not just a department—it is the foundation of our business. We enforce a Zero-Defect policy across all stages of production, from raw cotton selection to final packaging.

"We don't just inspect quality; we manufacture it."

In-House Testing Lab

Our on-site laboratory is equipped with advanced Uster Technologies equipment to perform rigorous tests:

  • Fiber Testing: Micronaire, Length, Strength using HVI.
  • Yarn Testing: Count (Ne), TPI, Tensile Strength, Regularity.
  • Fabric Testing: Color fastness, Shrinkage, Pilling, GSM check.
